# Changelog

Notable changes to SortBoard are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- One local-first board for Open, Closed, and Q-Sort workflows.
- Text, image, and short-video cards.
- Local project, media, and recording persistence with IndexedDB.
- Recording and replay of card movement and workflow-stage changes.
- Project backup and transfer through ZIP export and import.
- Unit, component, and Playwright end-to-end tests.

### Known limitations

- SortBoard is single-user, local-only pre-release software.
- Project ZIP files are backups, not a stable analysis format.
- Video playback and poster extraction depend on local codec support.
